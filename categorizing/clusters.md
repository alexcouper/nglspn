## Product Overview

Clusters is a word-grouping puzzle game inspired by the New York Times "Connections" format. Players are presented with a grid of words and must identify four groups of four words that share a hidden connection. The game differentiates itself by offering multiple difficulty levels and using OpenAI to generate its quizzes, providing a potentially unlimited supply of fresh puzzles. Built as a React single-page application with a Supabase backend, the site presents a clean, focused game interface. It is a side project that serves as both an entertainment product and a demonstration of AI-generated content for casual gaming.
