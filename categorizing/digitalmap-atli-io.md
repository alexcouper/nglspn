# digitalmap.atli.io

**URL:** https://digitalmap.atli.io/

## Database Description

Maps where Icelandic government agencies host their core digital infrastructure (email, websites, DNS) to understand dependencies. Uses publicly available DNS data to reveal which vendors and jurisdictions underpin government operations. Inspired by Proton's Europe Tech Watch.

## Product Overview

digitalmap.atli.io is a web-based dashboard that analyzes the digital infrastructure of 210 Icelandic public sector agencies sourced from island.is. Built as a Next.js application (generated with v0.app), it provides a visual analysis of where government agencies host their core digital services -- email, websites, and DNS -- by examining publicly available DNS records.

The dashboard presents data through multiple visualization cards, including charts showing the distribution of infrastructure providers across the three service categories (email, web hosting, and DNS). It includes breakdowns by vendor with progress-bar-style indicators showing market share, along with contextual summaries. A searchable and filterable data table allows users to explore individual agencies and see which providers each one relies on.

The project frames its analysis around digital sovereignty, highlighting dependencies on foreign cloud providers versus domestic Icelandic hosting. By mapping these vendor relationships, it reveals concentration risks and jurisdictional considerations for Iceland's public sector IT infrastructure -- for example, how much of government email flows through Microsoft 365, or how many agency websites are hosted outside Iceland.

## Possible Categories

- **Open Data & Transparency** - Takes publicly available DNS data and transforms it into an accessible analysis that reveals the hidden infrastructure dependencies of Iceland's government, promoting transparency around public sector technology choices.
- **Data & Analytics** - A data-driven dashboard with charts, tables, and breakdowns that provides analytical insight into the vendor landscape underpinning Icelandic government digital services.
- **Society Impact** - Raises important questions about digital sovereignty and the reliance of Icelandic public institutions on foreign technology providers, contributing to public discourse on national infrastructure policy.
- **Community Boosters** - Serves the Icelandic community by making government infrastructure decisions visible and understandable, empowering citizens and policymakers to have informed discussions about digital independence.
