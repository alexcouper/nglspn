## Product Overview

Eventa is a comprehensive digital event marketplace for Iceland that aggregates events from across the country into a single, browsable platform. Built as a Next.js web application (with PWA/mobile app support), it lets users discover events by category and location. The site covers a wide range of event categories including music (tonleikar), comedy and stand-up, art exhibitions, sports, outdoor and nature activities, conferences and business events, health and wellness, tourism, food and drink, community events, education, theatre, festivals, literature, and film. Users can find events happening in Reykjavik, Akureyri, Kopavogur, Hafnarfjordur, Keflavik, Selfoss, Akranes, and other locations across Iceland. The platform includes user accounts with personalized profiles to customize which events appear on the homepage, the ability to follow venues and artists for notifications about new events, event creation tools (for both public and private events), and a registration system for managing attendance at capacity-limited events. The product is developed by DRIP ehf. and positions itself as the go-to hub for event discovery in Iceland.

## Possible Categories

- **Events & Entertainment** - Core fit as an event discovery and listing platform for concerts, shows, festivals, and activities across Iceland.

- **Social & Community** - Enables following artists/venues, personalized feeds, and community event creation, functioning as a social layer around events.
- **Marketplace** - Acts as a two-sided marketplace connecting event organizers with attendees, with tools for event creation and registration management.
