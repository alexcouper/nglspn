## Database Description

Keep is a private timeline for your life - a quiet place to save the moments you don't want to forget. Dinners with friends, weekend trips, small wins, everyday milestones. You can tag people, browse a scrollable timeline. No algorithms, no likes, no noise. Built as PWA.

## Product Overview

*Note: Unable to visit the live site; this summary is based on available project metadata.*

Keep is a personal life-logging application that lets users record and revisit everyday moments in a private, chronological timeline. Rather than competing with social media, it deliberately removes social features like likes, followers, and algorithmic feeds, positioning itself as a quiet, intentional journaling space. Users can tag people in entries and browse their history through a scrollable timeline interface. Built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with Vue/Nuxt on the frontend, Supabase for the backend, and Tailwind for styling, it is designed to work seamlessly across devices without requiring a native app install. Hosted on Vercel, the product targets individuals who want to preserve personal memories without the pressure or performativity of social platforms. Its "In Development" and "Mature" tags suggest it is an actively maintained project that has reached a stable, usable state.
