## Product Overview

Leiksvæði is a community-driven platform for discovering and mapping playgrounds across Iceland. It features an interactive Mapbox-powered map with a searchable database of 75+ approved playground entries across municipalities like Reykjavík, Hafnarfjörður, and Akureyri. Each playground listing includes photos, detailed equipment inventories (slides, swings, climbing structures, sandboxes, etc.), age-appropriate options (toddler-friendly, baby swings), and facility details (seating, bathrooms, fencing). Users can submit and review playgrounds, making it a living community resource for families. Built with Next.js and TypeScript, with bilingual support (Icelandic/English).
