## Database Description

Breast cancer is the most common cancer among women worldwide. In Iceland, around 250 women are diagnosed every year — yet early detection dramatically improves outcomes. Mín Skimun changes that. It's a free app that guides you through a monthly breast self-examination in under 10 minutes. No login. No data leaving your phone. Content creator/PO: Helena Ýr, Developer: Rakel Björt.

## Product Overview

Mín Skimun ("My Screening") is a free, privacy-first Progressive Web App (PWA) that provides step-by-step guidance for performing a monthly breast self-examination. Built with React and Vite, the app is designed to be installed directly on a user's phone from the browser — no app store download required. It runs entirely on the device with no user accounts, no login, and no data transmitted to external servers.

The app is presented in Icelandic and uses a warm, approachable visual design (pink-themed, using the Nunito typeface) to make the self-examination process feel accessible rather than clinical. It includes a service worker for offline use, meaning users can follow the examination guide even without an internet connection. The manifest specifies portrait orientation, reinforcing that this is built as a mobile-first, phone-in-hand experience. The overall goal is to lower the barrier to regular breast self-checks, contributing to earlier detection of breast cancer in Iceland.
