# Naglasupan

**URL:** https://naglasupan.is

## Database Description

A project focused on encouraging software development in Iceland. Aims to encourage more building, provide accelerated feedback, promote adoption of built products. Tech Stack: Next.js, React, Django, Postgres, Scaleway, GitHub, Claude.

## Product Overview

Naglasupan is a community platform for Icelandic developers and builders to showcase their side projects and early-stage ideas. The site operates under the tagline "Byggjum, deilum, voxum saman" (Build, share, grow together) and serves as both a project directory and a competition platform. Users can submit projects for community review, browse other developers' work, and participate in competitions with prize pools. The platform hosts regular themed competitions -- for example, a "Kartafla" competition with a 50,000 ISK prize pool and a recently closed "Selleri" competition that attracted eight submissions. The site is built with a Next.js frontend and Django backend, and provides user accounts for project submission and community engagement. Its core purpose is to foster a culture of building within Iceland's tech community by providing visibility, feedback, and incentives for developers shipping products.

## Possible Categories

- **Community Boosters** - Directly aimed at growing and energising the Icelandic builder/developer community through shared visibility and encouragement.
- **Developer Tools & Platforms** - Functions as a platform for developers to gain exposure, feedback, and validation for their projects.
- **Social & Community** - Provides a social layer where builders can discover each other's work, engage through competitions, and grow together.
- **Education & Learning** - By showcasing real projects and encouraging participation, it serves as a learning resource for aspiring developers in Iceland.
