# Prótó

**URL:** https://proto.is

## Database Description

A free 4 week online creative challenge where participants prototype their ideas. First held October 2025. Signups open for October 2026. You can use Prótó as the kick in the pants you need to finally start that brilliant idea. Join someone else's project or get inspired. Share work, provide feedback, cheer each other on.

## Product Overview

Prótó ("Þín hugmynd að veruleika" — Your idea to reality) is an Icelandic online creative challenge platform built as a Next.js web application. It runs as a free, structured 4-week program where participants commit to prototyping and developing their creative ideas. The platform encourages people to take the leap on ideas they have been sitting on — whether starting their own project or joining someone else's. Participants share progress updates throughout the challenge, give and receive feedback, and support one another. The event runs annually (first held in October 2025, with signups open for October 2026), creating a time-boxed creative sprint that builds community around making things. The site is authored by Rúnar Vestmann and provides a blog/posts section ("pistlar") alongside the core challenge functionality.
