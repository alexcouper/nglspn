# rastimar.golf.is

**URL:** https://rastimar.golf.is/

## Database Description

Rástímar.is responds to a real need within the Icelandic golf community. Brings together info about golf simulators, courses, clubs, and PGA golf instructors. Helps golfers find available tee times. Hundreds of thousands of visits, tens of thousands of users. Icelandic Golf Association requested collaboration.

## Product Overview

Rástímar.is ("Tee Times") is a centralized platform for the Icelandic golf community that aggregates available tee times across all major golf courses in Iceland and golf simulators in the Reykjavik capital area. Built as a Remix web application deployed on Vercel, the site is organized around five main sections: tee time search for golf courses (Vellir), golf simulator availability (Golfhermar), golf instruction and PGA-certified coaches (Kennsla), tournament listings (Mot), and a beginner's guide to getting started in golf (Byrjadu i golfi). The platform solves a real fragmentation problem in the Icelandic golf ecosystem by bringing together booking information that was previously scattered across individual club websites. With hundreds of thousands of visits and tens of thousands of users, it has become a significant tool in the community, to the point where the Icelandic Golf Association has sought formal collaboration. The site uses a clean, modern design with Tailwind CSS and serves as both a practical booking aggregator and an informational hub for golfers at all levels.
