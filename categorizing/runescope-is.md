## Database Description

A deeper horoscope than the zodiac, based on the runes. A less boring way to learn languages, especially Icelandic. Bilingual tool (English/Icelandic). Practical reference for fortune tellers, readers, and creatives. Daily reflection system. Not finished, and never will be.

## Product Overview

RuneScope is a bilingual (English/Icelandic) web application that offers personalized rune readings based on the Elder Futhark runic system. The site allows users to discover their personal birth rune and fate rune by entering their birthday and time of birth. Built on Shopify and hosted at runescope.online, the product positions itself as a modern take on runic divination, offering a "deeper horoscope than the zodiac." Beyond fortune-telling, it doubles as a language-learning tool, using the runes and their meanings as a gateway into learning Icelandic. The site features a daily reflection system and serves as a practical reference for fortune tellers, rune readers, and creatives looking for inspiration from Norse runic traditions. The project is described as perpetually in progress.
