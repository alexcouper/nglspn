## Product Overview

*Note: Unable to visit the live site; this summary is based on available project metadata.*

Tensions is a web application that brings structured governance practices from Holacracy and sociocracy into a digital tool. In these organizational frameworks, a "tension" is the gap between the current state and a desired state -- surfacing and processing tensions is the primary mechanism for driving organizational change. The app likely provides features for defining and clarifying roles within a team or organization, capturing and tracking tensions as they arise, and facilitating structured tactical meetings where those tensions are processed according to defined protocols. Built with React, TypeScript, Firebase, and Firestore, it is a real-time collaborative tool where team members can participate in governance processes together. As a pre-production alpha, it is in early development and aimed at teams experimenting with self-management methodologies who want a lightweight, purpose-built tool rather than adapting generic project management software.
