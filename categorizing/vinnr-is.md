Vinnr.is is an AI-powered HR and employee training platform built for companies looking to streamline onboarding and ongoing employee development. The platform consolidates hiring workflows, training material delivery, and employee support into a single web application. Leveraging AWS Bedrock for its AI capabilities, the product appears targeted at small-to-medium businesses seeking an affordable, modern alternative to fragmented HR tooling. The tech stack (Django, PostgreSQL, Tailwind) and pre-seed/beta status suggest an early-stage Icelandic SaaS product focused on the Nordic market.
