## Product Overview

*Note: Unable to visit the live site; this summary is based on available project metadata.*

Whats for dinner is a Gemini AI-powered family meal planning assistant designed to take the daily stress out of deciding what to cook. The web app lets families describe their household (family size, dietary preferences, restrictions, etc.) and generates personalized dinner suggestions with easy-to-follow recipes. A key practical feature is automatic grocery list generation with approximate prices, which ties into the Icelandic grocery ecosystem and could serve as a companion tool to existing grocery store delivery services. The product targets busy families looking for a simple, AI-driven way to handle weekly meal planning without the cognitive overhead of browsing recipe sites or manually building shopping lists. As a side project currently in development, it demonstrates the use of Google's Gemini AI for a focused, consumer-friendly use case in the Icelandic market.
